DUNGEON DEPTHS — Pixel Art Pack
===========================================

A small, cohesive 16x16 pixel art asset pack for dungeon crawlers,
roguelikes, and game jams. Same limited-palette style and matching
mascot character as the Forest Adventure pack — mix and match freely.

WHAT'S INCLUDED
---------------
tiles/   6 tiles: stone floor, cracked floor, red rug/path, lava,
         stone pillar with torch, wooden crate
         (each provided at native 16x16 AND an upscaled crisp preview PNG)

sprites/ 8-frame character walk cycle (16x16, side-facing) —
         4 frames facing right (walk_0-3) + 4 mirrored frames
         facing left (walk_0_left-3_left)

ui/      3 HUD icons: heart, coin, key

preview/ cover_scene.png  — a sample scene built entirely from these assets
         asset_sheet.png  — every asset laid out on one sheet
